# GoWay

A freight marketplace for Uzbekistan. GoWay connects loads with trucks: a driver finds paid work
quickly, and a shipper gets a truck without a round of phone calls.

## How it works

Every load can be booked three ways, in one flow:

- **Instant offer.** The load goes to every eligible driver on the route at once. The first driver
  to accept takes it.
- **Load board.** Drivers browse open loads and subscribe to the routes they run.
- **Post and negotiate.** The shipper posts a load and drivers answer with their own price. The
  calculated price is a suggestion, not a rule.

When instant matching finds nobody, the load moves to the board instead of being cancelled.

GoWay handles both kinds of trips: on-demand moves inside a city, and scheduled routes between
regions.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Drivers** | Open a route, get loads on it, accept before anyone else, get paid to an in-app balance. |
| **Shippers** | Say what, from where, to where and when. See the price up front, follow the truck, pay by card or cash. |

The apps speak Uzbek (Latin and Cyrillic), Karakalpak, Russian, English and Chinese.
